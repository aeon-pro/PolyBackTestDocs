---
title: 'Snapshot at Timestamp'
openapi: 'GET /v3/{coin}/markets/{market_id}/snapshots/at/{timestamp}'
---

## What you get

The closest snapshot to `{timestamp}` - millisecond epoch or ISO-8601.

If no snapshot is within 2 seconds of the requested time, the response is 404 with `error.code = SNAPSHOT_NOT_FOUND`.

If `{timestamp}` is older than your plan's history window, the response is `403` with `error.code = HISTORY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`.

<Tip>Use this to align backtest decisions to a specific wall-clock moment without scanning a whole snapshot window.</Tip>
