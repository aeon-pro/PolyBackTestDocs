title: 'Get Market by Slug'
openapi: 'GET /v3/{coin}/markets/by-slug/{slug}'
---

## What you get

Same as Get Market, but keyed by URL-friendly slug.

<Info>Markets older than your plan's history window return `403 HISTORY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`.</Info>

<Tip>Slugs are stable per market; prefer them for any external link you control.</Tip>
